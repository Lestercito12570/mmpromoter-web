Frequently asked questions

How many people can use one subscription?

Two user seats are included in every base subscription. Additional seats are available for $5.99 per month or $64.69 per year per seat.

Does annual billing unlock more features?

No. We don't like or believe in a tiered access system. You subscribe, and you get everything. Annual billing simply saves you money.

How much do I save with annual billing?

The base subscription costs $269.99 annually instead of $299.88 over twelve monthly payments, a savings of $29.89. An additional annual seat costs $64.69 instead of $71.88 across twelve months.

What happens if I need more team members?

You can purchase additional user seats. Each added seat provides access to the same application features.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your subscription will remain active through the end of your current paid billing period, whether monthly or annual. Payments are non-refundable, and you won't be charged again after your subscription ends.
